Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

The Missile Man of India and the People's President

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist, author, teacher, and the 11th President of India.

About Dr. Kalam

Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. Coming from a humble background, he developed a strong interest in science and technology from an early age.

He played an important role in India's space and missile development programs. His contributions to India's civilian space programme and military missile development earned him the title "Missile Man of India."

Dr. Kalam became the 11th President of India in 2002. He was widely respected for his simplicity, humility, dedication to education, and his vision for India's future.

Key Milestones

1931

Born in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu.

1954

Graduated in Physics from Saint Joseph's College, Tiruchirappalli.

1960

Joined the Aeronautical Development Establishment after completing aerospace engineering.

1980

Played a major role in the successful launch of India's first indigenous Satellite Launch Vehicle.

1997

Received the Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian award.

2002

Became the 11th President of India.

2015

Passed away while delivering a lecture at IIM Shillong.

Legacy

Dr. Kalam continues to inspire millions of students and young people through his books, speeches, scientific achievements, and vision for a developed India. His life represents the importance of education, perseverance, humility, and service to the nation.
